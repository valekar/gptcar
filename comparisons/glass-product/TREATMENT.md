# Aura / Light Within

Three candidate structures:
1. Interior detail first, then shell and case.
2. Case opens before an exploded inspection.
3. Caustic announces the object above it.

Chosen: 2. This gives the subject a readable action, reversal and payoff in twenty seconds.

Logline: Fictional glass earbuds lift from their case, reveal their internal metal architecture, and reassemble on a light pulse.

Benchmark: learn material legibility, held performance and frame-size hierarchy from the style contract’s general historical craft references; take no specific characters, shot layouts, melodies or logos. Original scene code only; upstream demo sources were not read.

Shot, beat, camera and sound map:
- 0–5s: Low three-quarter push: strip sweep outlines a glass charging case; lid hinges into halo.
- 5–10s: Macro orbit: two transparent earbuds lift, internal gold traces and dark drivers read through glass.
- 10–15s: Exploded axial view: shell, driver and metal ring drift apart; one silent beat before the snap.
- 15–20s: Parts snap together, cyan pulse enters guides, caustic rings spread; slow declaration orbit.

Cue grid: 120 BPM; cuts at the listed section boundaries, small foley on each action. Original bell/pad motif, bass response, deliberate rests before the turn and final payoff. Ambient wind or room tone is quiet under the score. Two short breathing gaps are written into the event schedule. No narration or subtitles; all text is a scene label or practical sign. The main story must read without text.

Look proof: render 2, 10 and 17 seconds with the deterministic still renderer, inspect imagery and repair failures. All camera/light/state functions seek directly from time.
