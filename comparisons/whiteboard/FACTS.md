The +45, −7 and net +38 microseconds/day are rounded GPS clock relativistic shifts, relative to ground clocks. Source: NIST, Putting Einstein to the Test, https://www.nist.gov/atomic-clocks/a-powerful-tool-for-science/putting-einstein-test — states the motion effect loses 7 µs/day, lower gravity gains 45 µs/day, net 38 µs/day.
