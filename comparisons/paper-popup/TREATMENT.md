# Pip Beyond the Page

Three candidate structures:
1. A pull-tab bridge opens the path off the page.
2. Three pages shrink toward the real room.
3. Book opens, danger grows, sprite leaves.

Chosen: 1. This gives the subject a readable action, reversal and payoff in twenty seconds.

Logline: A paper sprite crosses two pop-up spreads and leaps off the last page into the desk world.

Benchmark: learn material legibility, held performance and frame-size hierarchy from the style contract’s general historical craft references; take no specific characters, shot layouts, melodies or logos. Original scene code only; upstream demo sources were not read.

Shot, beat, camera and sound map:
- 0–5s: Descending macro into opened book; meadow cards hinge up back to front, sprite wakes.
- 5–10s: Eye-level track: sprite crosses paper stream via pull-tab bridge; page begins to curl.
- 10–15s: Page turn changes to ochre hills; sprite climbs a folding stair to the book edge.
- 15–20s: Sprite leaps to wooden desk; camera rises to show both scales and book folds down. Music box cadence.

Cue grid: 100 BPM; cuts at the listed section boundaries, small foley on each action. Original bell/flute motif, bass response, deliberate rests before the turn and final payoff. Ambient wind or room tone is quiet under the score. Two short breathing gaps are written into the event schedule. No narration or subtitles; all text is a scene label or practical sign. The main story must read without text.

Look proof: render 2, 10 and 17 seconds with the deterministic still renderer, inspect imagery and repair failures. All camera/light/state functions seek directly from time.
