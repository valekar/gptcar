# Selective history sources

1997: IBM Deep Blue defeated reigning chess world champion Garry Kasparov in a match. Primary source https://www.ibm.com/history/deep-blue .
2016: AlphaGo defeated Lee Sedol in Seoul. Primary source https://deepmind.google/research/alphago/ .
2022: OpenAI introduced ChatGPT, 30 November 2022. Primary source https://openai.com/index/chatgpt/ .

Only these three milestones are represented in the 20-second study. Original toy characters are symbolic stand-ins, not depictions or logos of real products. No scores, model capability comparisons or complete 1997–2026 timeline is claimed. Dates are labels; the report card repeats them without assigning grades.
