# Values and representation

Actual World rows for 2014–2025 in assets/capacity.json extracted from original CSVs retained alongside it. Sources https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/installed-solar-pv-capacity and https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/cumulative-installed-wind-energy-capacity-gigawatts , IRENA / Our World in Data, CC BY4.

Identical unit in both series: 1 plate =50 GW, counts round(value/50), no more than 25 GW rounding error. Brass marks each tenth plate. Pieces physically arrive; no stack scales.
