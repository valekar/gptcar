# Scope

Real public-domain / CC0 bridge and ship photographs and exact attributions are in DATA_SOURCES.md and CREDITS. These are illustrative bridge-type prints, not assessments of those particular structures. No span, height, vessel envelope, clearance, safety verdict or engineering calculation is invented. The story's recommendation is to establish a real project's navigation envelope before bridge selection. It replaces the upstream numerical four-option test with a sourced visual type study.
