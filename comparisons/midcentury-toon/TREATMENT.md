# Pip, Ready to Go

Three candidate structures:
1. Show the clean floor then teach the setup.
2. Three numbered setup cards with one hand action each.
3. Wrong versus right split-screen.

Chosen: 2. This gives the subject a readable action, reversal and payoff in twenty seconds.

Logline: A geometric presenter sets a turquoise robot vacuum up in three clear steps: dock, connect, start.

Benchmark: learn material legibility, held performance and frame-size hierarchy from the style contract’s general historical craft references; take no specific characters, shot layouts, melodies or logos. Original scene code only; upstream demo sources were not read.

Shot, beat, camera and sound map:
- 0–5s: Medium presenter and product on cream ground: dock placed against wall. Flute and marimba phrase.
- 5–10s: Circular callout travels from phone to robot: connection rings join. New jazz chord.
- 10–15s: Overhead close-up: finger presses start; vacuum begins an orderly lane. Beat of silence before click.
- 15–20s: Pullback to clean floor pattern; presenter gives four-finger approval. All three medallions return.

Cue grid: 120 BPM; cuts at the listed section boundaries, small foley on each action. Original flute/marimba motif, bass response, deliberate rests before the turn and final payoff. Ambient wind or room tone is quiet under the score. Two short breathing gaps are written into the event schedule. No narration or subtitles; all text is a scene label or practical sign. The main story must read without text.

Look proof: render 2, 10 and 17 seconds with the deterministic still renderer, inspect imagery and repair failures. All camera/light/state functions seek directly from time.
