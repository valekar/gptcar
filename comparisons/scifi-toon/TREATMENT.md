# A Very Long Coffee Run

Three candidate structures:
1. Three increasingly costly coffee counters.
2. One portal that keeps returning the wrong cup.
3. A chase after a self-aware vending machine.

Chosen: 1. This gives the subject a readable action, reversal and payoff in twenty seconds.

Logline: A tired inventor and nervous assistant jump through strange universes for coffee, finally receiving a cup too large to carry.

Benchmark: learn material legibility, held performance and frame-size hierarchy from the style contract’s general historical craft references; take no specific characters, shot layouts, melodies or logos. Original scene code only; upstream demo sources were not read.

Shot, beat, camera and sound map:
- 0–4s: Medium two-shot in cluttered workshop: inventor points at empty mug. Muted organ figure and click.
- 4–8s: Portal rush to purple fungal café; a tentacled barista offers a tiny cup. Reaction close-up at 7.
- 8–12s: Swirl to coral desert cafe run by a many-eyed creature; steaming cup retreats. Accelerated chip notes.
- 12–16s: Beige normal counter; inventor finally smiles. Music disappears before reveal.
- 16–20s: Wide pullback: cup is house-sized, assistant looks at inventor. A single deadpan bass note ends it.

Cue grid: 120 BPM; cuts at the listed section boundaries, small foley on each action. Original organ/chip motif, bass response, deliberate rests before the turn and final payoff. Ambient wind or room tone is quiet under the score. Two short breathing gaps are written into the event schedule. No narration or subtitles; all text is a scene label or practical sign. The main story must read without text.

Look proof: render 2, 10 and 17 seconds with the deterministic still renderer, inspect imagery and repair failures. All camera/light/state functions seek directly from time.
