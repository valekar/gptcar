# Evidence and limits

Actual registered NASA Terra/MODIS plates: 25 August 2000 (`2000238`) and 19 August 2014 (`2014231`). Source: https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/images/84437/the-aral-sea-loses-its-eastern-lobe . NASA Earth Observatory/Jesse Allen, public-domain imagery. The original plate includes the agency's historic coastline overlay; this is retained unchanged.

The displayed statement “eastern lobe … dry” is the observation and source article's finding for 2014. No water mask, area, altitude or geospatial distance is invented. This compact study compares the prepared NASA plates in source-image coordinates; it does not reproduce the upstream multi-decade Landsat measurement or recovery claim. No globe texture is fabricated.
