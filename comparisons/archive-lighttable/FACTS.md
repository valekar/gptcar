# Catalogue truth

Actual scans from Library of Congress Wright Brothers glass negatives. Images keep source proportions and original damage; negatives are computed transmission representations of the actual supplied positive scans. The loupe restores the real positive. No generated archive content.

- `ppprs.00626`: 17 December 1903, first flight, 120 feet in 12 seconds, 10:35 a.m. Record https://www.loc.gov/item/00652085/ . Photographers credited Wilbur/Orville Wright and John T. Daniels.
- `ppprs.00629`: fourth and final 17 December 1903 flight, 852 feet in 59 seconds. Record https://www.loc.gov/item/2001696559/ . Wright brothers.
- `ppprs.00610`: Wilbur in the damaged machine after the unsuccessful 14 December 1903 trial. Record https://www.loc.gov/item/2001696253/ . Wright brothers.

These primary catalogue records were verified for this film. The displayed durations are the catalogue's figures, not measured from the still photos. This study uses the supplied public-domain historic 1903 photographs and the first/fourth-flight catalogue comparison; it does not reproduce the upstream telegram/newspaper 57-versus-59 story because those original document scans were not supplied. Catalogue rights lines say no known restrictions; public-domain status here relies on the historical works' expired copyright, not treating that phrase as a CC0 license.
