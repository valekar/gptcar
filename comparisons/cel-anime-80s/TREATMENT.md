# Before the First Light

Three candidate structures:
1. Night ride followed by rooftop delivery.
2. Start with tape insert, rewind through its route.
3. Alternate courier close-ups and the launch clock.

Chosen: 1. This gives the subject a readable action, reversal and payoff in twenty seconds.

Logline: A courier carries a cassette across a rain-wet neon city and slots it into a rooftop launch transmitter before dawn.

Benchmark: learn material legibility, held performance and frame-size hierarchy from the style contract’s general historical craft references; take no specific characters, shot layouts, melodies or logos. Original scene code only; upstream demo sources were not read.

Shot, beat, camera and sound map:
- 0–5s: Low side track of motorcycle in rain, painted parallax city. FM arpeggio and bass establish pulse.
- 5–9s: Held three-quarter portrait, light bands cross visor and hair; tape is visible on chest. Drum machine thins.
- 9–14s: Rear rush through a neon street, then two-frame radial impact at the rooftop arrival. Rising synth brass.
- 14–20s: Macro cassette enters transmitter; crane to rocket flare against dawn. Bell cadence and wind.

Cue grid: 120 BPM; cuts at the listed section boundaries, small foley on each action. Original chip/brass motif, bass response, deliberate rests before the turn and final payoff. Ambient wind or room tone is quiet under the score. Two short breathing gaps are written into the event schedule. No narration or subtitles; all text is a scene label or practical sign. The main story must read without text.

Look proof: render 2, 10 and 17 seconds with the deterministic still renderer, inspect imagery and repair failures. All camera/light/state functions seek directly from time.
