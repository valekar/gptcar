# One Spare Stud

Three candidate structures:
1. Build, failed ignition, successful launch.
2. Start at moon then show the build.
3. A spare part becomes the unexpected engine.

Chosen: 1. This gives the subject a readable action, reversal and payoff in twenty seconds.

Logline: A brick astronaut assembles a rocket from desk scraps and lands beside a brick moon.

Benchmark: learn material legibility, held performance and frame-size hierarchy from the style contract’s general historical craft references; take no specific characters, shot layouts, melodies or logos. Original scene code only; upstream demo sources were not read.

Shot, beat, camera and sound map:
- 0–5s: Stud macro moves to astronaut and workbench; rigid pieces arc into a rocket with clicks.
- 5–9s: Low medium: engine sputters, one brick slips; astronaut fits the spare blue stud. Music stops at failure.
- 9–14s: Tracking rise: brick flames and smoke stack, rocket launches above books.
- 14–20s: Moon landing at toy-eye level, then high desk reveal. A bright toy melody resolves.

Cue grid: 120 BPM; cuts at the listed section boundaries, small foley on each action. Original bell/pluck motif, bass response, deliberate rests before the turn and final payoff. Ambient wind or room tone is quiet under the score. Two short breathing gaps are written into the event schedule. No narration or subtitles; all text is a scene label or practical sign. The main story must read without text.

Look proof: render 2, 10 and 17 seconds with the deterministic still renderer, inspect imagery and repair failures. All camera/light/state functions seek directly from time.
