# Sugar Takes the Stage

Three candidate structures:
1. A tabletop relay around kitchen instruments.
2. A single stretching-arm chase through a cupboard.
3. A stage chase that ends in a shared dance.

Chosen: 3. This gives the subject a readable action, reversal and payoff in twenty seconds.

Logline: A coffee cup pursues a sugar cube through the kitchen; the cube turns the chase into a dance.

Benchmark: learn material legibility, held performance and frame-size hierarchy from the style contract’s general historical craft references; take no specific characters, shot layouts, melodies or logos. Original scene code only; upstream demo sources were not read.

Shot, beat, camera and sound map:
- 0–4s: Medium proscenium: sugar vaults from the saucer; cup recoils. A brushed chord and click mark the leap.
- 4–9s: Side truck along the checker counter: cup runs; the sugar conducts spoons. Syncopated brass follows steps.
- 9–14s: Tight reaction, then high-angle reach: a noodle arm catches the hanging sugar. Band stops at 12.5.
- 14–20s: Pull to ensemble: sugar drops into the cup; steam makes a heart; the cup grabs the iris. A hot-band cadence.

Cue grid: 144 BPM; cuts at the listed section boundaries, small foley on each action. Original brass/organ motif, bass response, deliberate rests before the turn and final payoff.
