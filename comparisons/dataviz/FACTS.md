# Real data and method

Source: https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/tabledata_v4/GLB.Ts+dSST.csv ; documentation https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/ . Accessed 2026-10-18. Frozen original CSV included.

Selected columns by name: Year and JJA. Selected inclusive years 1925–2024 = 100 June–August seasons. Global land-ocean temperature anomaly in degrees Celsius, relative to 1951–1980 mean, taken directly from the published JJA field, no invented samples or smoothing. 2024 JJA anomaly in this snapshot is +1.24 °C. Colour is a symmetric diverging ramp over ±1.5 °C, zero centred. Lines and stripes preserve all 100 values.

GISTEMP Team, 2026: GISS Surface Temperature Analysis (GISTEMP), version 4. NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies. Lenssen et al. 2024, A GISTEMPv4 observational uncertainty ensemble, JGR Atmospheres 129, e2023JD040179, doi:10.1029/2023JD040179. US-government data, public domain; acknowledgment NASA GISS/GISTEMP.
