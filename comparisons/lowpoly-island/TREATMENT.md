# Notes from the Sea

Three candidate structures:
1. Grow from one tile in an expanding spiral.
2. Two islands join across water.
3. One finished house creates the world around it.

Chosen: 1. This gives the subject a readable action, reversal and payoff in twenty seconds.

Logline: A sea sprouts hexagonal land and a small village on musical notes, then its windows illuminate under stars.

Benchmark: learn material legibility, held performance and frame-size hierarchy from the style contract’s general historical craft references; take no specific characters, shot layouts, melodies or logos. Original scene code only; upstream demo sources were not read.

Shot, beat, camera and sound map:
- 0–5s: Orthographic close-up: first hexagons breach faceted water on individual marimba notes.
- 5–10s: Pure zoom to a village as walls and roofs drop and bounce.
- 10–15s: Turntable drifts toward high map view; trees and a windmill pop on eighth notes.
- 15–20s: Dusk changes facet colours; window emissives ignite one by one, sky gains stars. Pullback with bell motif.

Cue grid: 120 BPM; cuts at the listed section boundaries, small foley on each action. Original marimba/bell motif, bass response, deliberate rests before the turn and final payoff. Ambient wind or room tone is quiet under the score. Two short breathing gaps are written into the event schedule. No narration or subtitles; all text is a scene label or practical sign. The main story must read without text.

Look proof: render 2, 10 and 17 seconds with the deterministic still renderer, inspect imagery and repair failures. All camera/light/state functions seek directly from time.
