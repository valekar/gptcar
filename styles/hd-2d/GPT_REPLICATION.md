# Lessons from full reference reconstruction

Treat this as a source-informed reconstruction when reusable pixel, sprite, prop and environment factories are used. Disclose them explicitly; visual closeness is not a blind same-prompt benchmark. Write new complete scene, camera and performance assembly rather than routing through original set modules.

Preserve the published tilt-shift cut:60 fps delivery,1920×1080 production, SSAA 2, physical signed DOF plus a tracked tilt band. Fixed band centers serve harbor wides; project the hero’s actual world position for character shots. Keep nearest-filtered texture density and tiny pixel silhouettes while all cameras and lighting update smoothly.

Read and inspect the full journey. The forest must retain real trunks, dense fern/grass layers, fallen log, cyan mushrooms, moon shafts and near-tree occlusion. The cliff must retain two stone stair flights, huge actual depth, rain, both lightning curves, the exact long near-black protected-ember beat, kneel/stand poses and gradual relight. A generic glowing platform cannot carry these beats.

Raw prop shadow defaults matter. A tiny unshadowed brazier bowl can cast a huge incorrect ground shadow if an assembly helper silently enables castShadow. Preserve the individual prop’s intended light/shadow role. Keep lantern lights 0.42–0.45 meters toward the camera so the sprite stays readable; cap lighting where needed.

The lens climax needs the actual banded Fresnel cylinder, pedestal and fire inlet, twelve mullions, rear glass, iron balcony/rails, hatch, conical roof, tower body, headland, sea and clouds. Check preglow, pour and exact 53.5 ignition in separate frames, then the accelerating beam and rising pullback. Preserve the keeper’s complete typed quote and terminal caret before departure.

Recreate the score’s whole orchestral arc with explicit musical gain points after the room return: lamp-out breath, hard storm stop into a tail, near-silent D pedal, monotonic dominant rebuild, inhale, exact D-to-G-flat ignition and E-flat final arrival. Keep material Foley separate from musical gain automation. New speech/music must be credited as newly performed, and the original source recording must not be relabeled as new output.
