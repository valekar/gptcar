# Reference-matched paper film guidance

A paper-popup film is a physical theatre on a real desk. Preserve the relationship among metre-scale book, printed card thickness/borders, actual prop geometry, shallow focus and warm room lighting. Inspect the entire film before building: this example is 133 seconds at a published 60 fps, rather than a short catalogue loop. Keep all page turns, fifteen narration cues, bilingual chapters and bubbles, Crumple's two stomps, kind smoothing, Fold's transformation, sea/whale, physical night slats, reverse camera, leap/catch and long desk resolution.

Write independent card artwork and actor/set assembly. Generic fibre grain, alpha-depth card backs, hinged book/page deformation and physical depth-of-field helpers can be attributed and reused. Do not import upstream original character drawings or scene modules. Inventory exact shared assets, not an entire mutable asset parent directory.

Set cut borders from the card's smaller pixel dimension: a wide wave strip must not inherit a border sized for its width, which hides the boat and fills the scene with cream. Match front-strip heights and depth ordering so the red hull, sail and Pip remain visible between layers. Keep foreground foliage clear of central gestures. Make door and sky-slat turns physical rotations. Put hanging stars in front of the panels and within the shot frustum, with real threads and descent.

For the reverse angle, match the page-edge character position before setting a shallow focus distance; a displacement of a few centimetres changes apparent scale and blur dramatically. Generate full-resolution stills for every action and all transitions, then inspect the complete sequence. A successful render does not establish scene fidelity. Preserve complete audio structure with fresh narration, an independently composed sectional sampled score, and material-specific paper/wood/flight sounds.

Independent comparison lessons: trigger small front flowers and shrubs during the opening, before the tall scenery finishes rising. Woodland mushrooms need thin wild stems and compact clustered caps; reusing the mushroom-house silhouette changes the set identity. The decisive stomp must visibly flatten Crumple beneath Pip at the impact frame and carry a large double-outlined action word/starburst.

Draw long foam strips in isotropic physical pixels rather than stretching a square drawing context. An anisotropic stroke becomes an excessively thick dark wedge on a steep scallop. Preserve bold ivory cut edges, thin ink and deep scalloped curvature across multiple overlapping rows. Keep the boat hull visible between the lower front rows. Match typography by actual font metrics and outline width: this reference's chapters use84 px Lilita One/12 px ink, English subtitles40 px and Chinese32 px, with paper double borders. Broadly similar small text is visibly weaker in comparison.
