# Microscope reference recreation guidance

For an explicit matched recreation, preserve the original whole-slide file, hash, registration, circle centres, diameter and threshold. A different crop from the same file cannot support the original measured story. Restore the data-only preparation first and record the actual derivative hashes. Keep the source renderer untouched and independently author the composition.

Describe every scale transition, reading, focus event and title before coding. Store measured reference compositions as camera data and apply them with a continuous similarity transform, including roll. Select registered pyramid plates by pixel ratio and crop coverage. Compare the actual objective transition times; a close-looking camera can still trigger the wrong magnification during a reading.

Every mark and counted mask must use the same world-to-screen transform as the specimen. Reveal the actual threshold mask behind a screen-aligned scan front, clipped to the exact physical circle. Use the cumulative measured scan curve for the rolling value. Keep the raw count, threshold method and final landing value readable. Do not paint invented collagen pixels or replace tissue with schematic cells.

Review the whole sequence and the short events between regular samples: blend into each plate, the two moving scan fronts, both circles before the verdict, the nucleus focal sweep and ring, and the six-step climb. Include the photographed slide and its label in the opening and ending. The typography, source panel and calibrated instruments are part of the scene.

Recompose the full score with the original tempo, chord direction, instrument family and silence windows. Generate instrument cues from the actual new plate changes. Retain every original spoken word with freshly synthesized voice. Distinguish an image measurement under the stated threshold from clinical interpretation.
