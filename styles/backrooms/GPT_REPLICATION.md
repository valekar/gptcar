# Lessons from a complete reference reconstruction

The recording medium carries the plot. Reconstruct every camera position/orientation/focus/aperture/fear key, then map all planning times through one documented warp. Validate final mapped values directly: three flickers 27.15/27.65/28.15; presence focus 32.85–33.35; tear 36.05–36.7; hush 46.75–47.75; door 50.18125–51.29167; REC-off 56.4. Do not preserve an unwarped approximate time from prose over the numerical source table.

Inspect the full original and supplement one-second sampling with fast focus, flicker, tape and door targets. Enlarge the far presence for analysis only: the final film must keep it tiny and ambiguous. Preserve the organic thin figure and wave, its dead-light zone, local dark fog and AO exclusion. Never replace it with a close monster or geometric stick figure.

Rebuild actual geometry and a float-texture area-emitter field. Generic texture/light-field material, shoe/figure, VHS and bitmap primitives may be reused with attribution; complete scene, light grid and camera/story/media choreography must be new. Explicitly call the result source-informed reconstruction, not a blind same-prompt benchmark.

Load all seven IBM font weights before constructing notice textures. Upstream font declarations may point to missing ignored binaries; restore actual local font files and inspect the printed rule wrapping at full size. Read the second notice in a low, front-on camera. Preserve rule-two/rule-five contradiction and the final printed command while PA speaks only Rule six.

Keep exported caption clauses/cues separate from whole voice inputs. Do not retime a correct caption to a newly generated take. Bitmap OSD avoids barrel distortion but shares tape tracking; closed captions live after the VHS pass. Check the clock jump, battery drain/blink and REC switch as narrative events.

Recreate only recorded-world sound: fluorescent harmonics, camera/AF/zoom, carpet/body/door matter, quiet speech and filtered distant hold music. Follow tape-speed drift, gate light sounds with the picture, and force a full digital-zero second including tails. End program sound after REC-off, retaining only the source-like faint blank-tape hiss. Preserve all ten exact voice inputs with calm PA and close operator treatment. A score sting would replace the film’s actual mechanism.

## Tape grain verification

Inspect amplified gain in the dark corridor at 27–36 seconds. On some GPU paths a sin-dot floating-point noise hash creates diagonal correlation that resembles a lighting or AO error. Test AO independently before changing scene lighting. A deterministic arithmetic hash can preserve fine tape grain without coherent bands; retain the original AO settings when AO is not the cause.
