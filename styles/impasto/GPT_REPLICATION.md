# Reference-matched impasto production

Preserve the entire shot table and score grid before painting. The library's generic height-field renderer, confined-stroke conversion and reusable models are legitimate shared tools; list their paths and hashes and distinguish reused model geometry from newly drawn sets and scene assembly. Never import the original film/scene renderer.

Review macro, acting and overhead shots at full delivery size. A monochrome boot can disappear against an arcade of the same value: establish a calm ground plane, sufficiently large boot silhouette and explicit puddle/splash under the contact. Keep the camera inside every reference plate at all zoom/rotation extrema. A shrinking mandala must still show route paint; use permanent accumulated broad knife dabs, then crane out without exposing empty backing.

Every colour event belongs to a specific material and cue: red canopy first, coat following, cello on bow contact, nine crowd pops one beat apart, whole square only after the grand pause. Freeze all visual time during the pause, and gate the final mix across all buses and reverb tails to digital zero. No narration means the full solo-to-musette-to-resolve score arc must carry the story.
