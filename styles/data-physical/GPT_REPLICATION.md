# Reference-matched physical data guidance

Preserve the exact original archived datasets and same years before building any object. This film uses IRENA 2026 / OWID World 2014–2025, not a newer cumulative-capacity revision. Keep both series on the same 10-GW physical unit; every tenth piece becomes brass as a ruler marker. A brass plate remains one 10-GW piece. State that distinction in FACTS.md while preserving the original narration/label wording for visual comparison.

Rebuild every physical object in Three.js. The reference has1935 plates, a real walnut dial with two inlaid spiral paths/year ticks, aluminium solar, transmissive teal acrylic wind and brass every tenth plate, plus an engraved final puck. Use original dimensional/material/light/camera measurements as reference data; independently author assembly, per-piece state and scene control. Generic postprocessing and math can be attributed; importing the original set/film/timeline is not a new scene.

Do not condense the continuous nine-beat arc. Hold the 2014 pair before 1.95, show the physical unit rule, walk through all intermediate years, hold four 2020/2021 readings before the crossover verdict, then feed all 52 actual 2025 new plates before the dial/puck reveal. Counter values must follow landed pieces rather than elapsed time. Pin the counter leader to the rightmost actual plate corner, not the stack centre, or the number collides with the object during the crane. Check year orientation in the final camera, not just on the canvas.

Use exact bilingual type roles/weights and Chinese-first subtitles. Recreate the full 96-BPM sectional score with fresh sampled instruments, material-specific landing cues, pitched brass ruler events and brief pre-impact silences. Keep the two designed crossover/puck accents distinct. Itemize exact shared asset files instead of a mutable whole asset directory. Full matched-time still review must cover every major scene and each short feed/impact event before the source-ready manifest is written last.

At the final dial distance, thin plate edges can alias after output resizing. Inspect the actual delivery resolution as well as close-ups. Keep the real object count and geometry, reduce excess independent jitter if needed, and use a light scene-only antialias pass; keep overlay type sharp. A white engraving texture multiplied by brass produces the intended puck finish; a brass-coloured texture multiplied by the same brass material becomes too dark. Check both concentric engraving rings and all120 tick marks at the final camera.
