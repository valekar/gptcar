# Replicating a published engraving film

Retain the full specimen geometry and closed wing cells; hatching, fur and three scientific inset drawings are the artwork, not optional texture. Reuse credited generic line/copper/plate/wash engines and subject drawings, but independently stage all scene/render code. Audit the entire original sequence and dedicated exact ring/burnish/fly/peel/color cues.

The copper guide must be faint contour-only, never all completed hatching. Calibrate the burin and swarf in screen coordinates: undersizing makes the handle a tiny prop. Keep washes below ink; only bow strings when color arrives. Separate full-PCM silence from music-only rests. Scientific names can yield ASR aliases; retain the exact script and disclose normalization rather than changing words. Every numeral, note, label and natural-size specimen needs its original readable hold. Font files must be local and awaited before building engravings.
