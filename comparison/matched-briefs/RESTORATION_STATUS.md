# Reference input restoration

The reconstruction uses the published film's subjects, acquisition windows and measurements. Restoring licensed underlying data supports new GPT scene code; it does not turn the original scene implementation or movie into new output.

- **Microscope:** complete Ventana-1 BIF, 227,377,284 bytes, SHA-256 `2eb05cd8f628c36408c3fde09beeeebaaae90181c4f840d5475ec7594bdcbbb6`. Original ROIs reproduce 13.5% and 32.6% collagen, 1.41 mm separation, and an 8.521 µm fitted nucleus diameter.
- **LiDAR:** original USGS CT 2016 crop and filtering reproduce 4,789,797 returns, 1,319,669 ground returns and 3,425.5 m of wall traces. Original 2016 NAIP tile restored.
- **Archive:** all five original records restored from LoC TIFF masters and the attributed telegram scan; processed photographic layers regenerated.
- **Teardown:** original Corne CAD commit `63366fb7f51cf2a796462fdd90527e99dd126055`, PCB/plate data and case STEP tessellation restored.
- **White model:** the published 3,887-building, 8,860-part, 108-deck geometry and statistics remain unchanged. Water mask regenerated from BGT records; shadow mask regenerated from the preserved geometry and published sun position. Small raster boundaries can vary; these are disclosed rather than presented as byte-identical missing originals.
- **Briefing board:** the original six source photographs and object cutouts have been regenerated, with source credits preserved. Segmentation pixels can vary from the missing original masks.
- **Satellite briefing:** exact original main/fill scene IDs have been recovered and pinned. Final plate preparation and measurement reconciliation are still in progress. The published movie and current source have two conflicting historical percentage labels; these must be disclosed and reconciled before acceptance.
- **Data physicalization:** the original CSV, source values and 10 GW sheet units remain available; no different dataset or coarser replacement units are accepted.

Large analytical caches stay outside the repository. The film's CREDITS/FACTS and the shared-input release manifest identify the deliverable inputs. Each completed movie still requires independent scene and audio review.
