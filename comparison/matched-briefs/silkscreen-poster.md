## Materials

Opaque hard-edged silkscreen on cream #F2E8D2 stock. Six flat inks per poster; stepped sky bands, no gradients or line outlines. Fine pinholes, modest mottle, 1–3 px registration slips and filled edge traps. The workbench has dark wooden planks, grain, knots, muted stains and tape corners. Hinged aluminium/wood screen shows mesh and ring shadow; rounded viscous ink bead and squeegee blade drive every reveal. Big Shoulders Display 900 names and Outfit 600 tracked labels are printed ink. Posters are 1000×1500 units with 26-unit borders, art to y1140 and dark information band y1148–1474.
