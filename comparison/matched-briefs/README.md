# Full-film reconstruction briefs

All51 published films were inspected across their full duration, with additional exact frames at short inserts and transitions. Each brief preserves the story, scene order, on-screen copy, narration and important sound cues before new scene code is written.

The original conversation prompts were not published. Quoted STYLE/DEMO/source instructions are distinguished from reconstructed shot descriptions; these documents do not claim to recover private chat history. Newly recreated speech and music are required.
