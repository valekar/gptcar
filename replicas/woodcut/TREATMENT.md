## Materials

Black inked woodblock; white is carved-away light, not white pen outlining. U/V-gouge cuts with chipped edges, directional grain and curved hatching describing volume; cream laid paper, two-frame misregistration on cuts. One copper plate only where carved white permits it. Founder old bearded man, boy knit pompom hat; detailed sculpted hands rendered to woodcut. Forge, crucible, bell mould, rope, compass and full snowbound valley.
