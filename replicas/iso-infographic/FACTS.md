# Illustrative coffee journey

The 1,600 m farm, 21 drying days, 60 kg sack / 400,000 beans, 14,000 ship containers, 18 sea days, 12 roasting minutes at 220 °C, and approximately 70 beans per cup are frozen illustrative inputs from the published story. They are not new observations, universal processing standards, or a verified real shipment.

The story's arithmetic is preserved exactly: 400 km of origin road + 10,500 km at sea + 100 km of destination road = 11,000 km. The final bar has eleven cells of 1,000 km, with 500 km of road shown proportionally in gold and 10,500 km of sea in blue. The square board is explicitly not to scale. The six narration lines reproduce the archived claims rather than introducing new factual assertions.
