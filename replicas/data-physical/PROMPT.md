## Sound direction and recreation

96 BPM, 4/4, D major; one chord per bar: Dadd 9 – Dadd 9 – Bm – Gmaj 7 | D – A/C♯ – Bm – G | Asus (bar 8, cut short) | D – Bm – G | A – Gmaj 7 | D – D.

| Section | Time | Palette | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wind leads | 0–10 | glass harp + harp motif (A 5 F♯5 E 5 D 5…), synth pad, bowed vibraphone | sparse; the plates' own ticks and brass notes carry the rhythm |
| Growth | 10–20 | marimba arpeggio in 8 ths, pulse bass in quarters (2 nd/3 rd harmonics) | each pair's cascade is an arpeggio of ticks and glockenspiel / glass notes climbing a D pentatonic ladder (one note per 100 GW) |
| Crossover | 20–21.875 | timpani heartbeat on A, a held horn A 3 | suck-out 21.5–21.875 (music and foley −30 dB) |
| Turn | 21.875 | punch (60–120 Hz) + bass drum + timpani D 3 + horns D–F♯–A + trombone D 2 + glockenspiel F♯6 + metal ring | second-loudest moment |
| 2025 | 22.5–30 | marimba 16 ths, pulse 8 ths, a glockenspiel line D 6–E 6–F♯6–A 6–B 6–A 6 over the feed; 52 metal ticks; vibraphone chord + timpani when the last plate lands (29.2) | the densest stretch |
| Dial | 30–33.75 | horns D 3/A 3/F♯4 and cellos D 3 under the crane, marimba back to 8 ths | suck-out 33.75–34.375 |
| Puck | 34.375 | punch + bass drum + gran cassa + timpani D 3 + tubular bells D 5/A 4 + metal rings; five small ticks of the puck settling | the loudest moment of the film |
| End | 34.4–39.4 | harp arpeggio D 4…D 6, bowed vibraphone D 5/A 4 | fade |
