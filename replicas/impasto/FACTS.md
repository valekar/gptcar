# Provenance and limits

This is a newly assembled reference-matched recreation of the published 952-frame film, not a recovered original chat prompt or an independently modeled copy of every shared character. The MIT generic knife engine and reusable cellist/umbrella models are explicitly shared and hashed. All set plates, girl model, world assembly, cameras, choreography and music note sequence are newly authored. The original scene renderer, original video pixels and original audio are not in the render path.

Delivery is 1280×720, 24 fps, 952 frames (39.6666667 s). Internal artwork is 1920×1080 with a 2× supersampled height field. The complete original nine-shot story, exact film title, no-narration choice, minor-to-major musical form, nine pitched umbrella events and grand pause are retained.

Known geometric variation: independently drawn plaza proportions, girl face/body, cobble fan arrangement, pedestrian blocking, umbrella palette assignment and circular route geometry differ from the original. Material-specific heights, opaque knife planes, synchronized grey-to-colour events, bow acting, boot splash, persistent trails and all camera classes are retained. This is not pixel identity. Fresh score preserves documented harmony/tempo/cues, with a newly composed melody.
