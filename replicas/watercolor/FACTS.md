# Factual scope and original-script provenance

This reconstruction preserves the published original script, not an independently newly researched documentary script. Exact thirteen English lines and Chinese subtitle cards are recorded in comparison/matched-briefs/watercolor.json with source hashes. No invented additional measurements are introduced.

The original credits attribute rounded rainfall and vegetation figures to standard Australian botanical/ecological references, including Australia's State of the Forests reports and Australian National Botanic Gardens, without providing per-claim URLs. The script's claims are: driest inhabited continent; red-centre rainfall<250mm; spinifex about1/5land; eucalypts>800species/about3/4forests; epicormic regeneration; mountain ash tallest flowering plant/~100m; rainforest<1%land; widespread rainforest~50Ma. These are retained as the comparison's original narrated claims, with the original source limitations disclosed.

Coastline geometry is Natural Earth public-domain data via world-atlas2.0.2 ISC. The~90rainfall input values are the original manually typed approximate long-term station values, openly identified as schematic. We independently interpolate them into painted bands. The50Ma→today transformation uniformly scales that contemporary schematic field; it is an illustrative metaphor for drying, NOT an actual palaeoclimate reconstruction. No past rainfall or vegetation-area percentage is measured from this animation.

The tree ruler is approximate illustrative scale; no claim that the displayed procedural specimen is a survey of a real individual tree. Animal and plant drawings are procedural, not documentary photographic evidence.
