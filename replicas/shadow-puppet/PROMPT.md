## Materials

Transmitted lamp light through woven cloth; patterned translucent carved leather, not black cardboard. Hou Yi has eleven articulated pieces, fish-scale armour, red headdress, long curved plume, rods/joints and profile face. Ten flame-rimmed suns each with crow emblem. Scenery has lattice hut, meander rail, contour-carved hills/tree. Red-gold wooden proscenium and audience silhouettes; backstage hands/lamp reveal.
